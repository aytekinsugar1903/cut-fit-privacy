CUT & FIT privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This policy applies to CUT & FIT 1.0.0 and later.

Data we collect

CUT & FIT is a plank-cutting puzzle. It does not create accounts, show ads or connect to the internet. It does not request location, contacts, photos or the advertising ID, and it does not send game data to the developer.

Only the following stay on your phone:

  • Level progress, scores and best cuts
  • An optional nickname (up to 12 characters)
  • Mute on/off preference

These live in the app's private folder. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Sound and vibration

Cut and SNAP sounds are bundled in the game; the microphone is not used. Vibration permission is only for the short haptic at cut and fit moments.

Children

The game has no ads or chat. The score list is only the records on this device. No personal data is collected from children or any other users.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page with a new date.
